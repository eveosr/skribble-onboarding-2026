Leave & Time Off

Life happens. We've got you.

Beyond statutory leave, Skribble has introduced additional flexibility to help you manage life's responsibilities while maintaining strong performance.

⏱
Time Off
For minor disruptions.
Short periods (generally up to 2 hours) for situations that don't require a full day of leave. No need to use annual leave for every small life event.
Examples
· Bank visits or government admin
· Car breakdown / traffic disruption
· School drop-off when a spouse is unavailable
· Doctor's appointment (short)
⚠ Not intended to extend holidays or replace annual leave. Missing half a day = use annual leave.
🔄
Replacement Leave
For effort beyond hours.
When business requirements ask you to work outside normal hours — and manager approval has been obtained — you earn replacement leave in return.
Most common in
· Events & activations
· Productions & launches
· Client-facing projects with extended timelines
We don't encourage overtime — but when it's needed, your additional commitment is recognised.
